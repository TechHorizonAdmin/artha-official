Product and release disclosures

Artha is presented with clear product and release information. Feature presence in source, screenshots or demonstrations must not be interpreted as a certification that a related provider, device, platform or regulated workflow is live and accepted.

Company status

Artha Business Suite is the product brand. Company incorporation is in progress. The approved legal entity name, CIN, tax registrations and binding customer terms will be published after issuance and professional review.

External services

Payments, GST-provider submission, AI, messaging, hosting, domains and other integrations require separately configured accounts, credentials, permissions and, where applicable, provider acceptance. Their current commercial configuration is not disclosed on this public website.

Platform distribution

The current r4 downloads are evaluation artifacts. macOS Developer ID signing and notarisation, Windows Authenticode, Android production signing and store review, Linux release signing, target-device acceptance and authorised release approval remain separate gates.

Legal and commercial matters

Company owner/entity details, intellectual-property records, trademark/domain rights, customer terms, pricing, data-processing terms and product availability commitments should be maintained as company records. This page is not legal, tax, accounting or investment advice.

Product evolution

Capabilities, release status and documentation may evolve. Operators should rely on the relevant product documentation, signed service documentation and their own operating requirements for decisions.
