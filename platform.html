Platform overview

A serious foundation for modern business operations.

Artha Business Suite is a multi-industry operating platform spanning sales, inventory, purchasing, finance and GST preparation, customers, teams, reporting, merchant commerce and specialised operational engines.

Release posture

The current build passes local type, contract, quality and web-export gates plus clean staging replay, tenant isolation, retail lifecycle and specialised-engine smoke journeys. Publisher signing, clean-device acceptance, provider activation, hardware certification and statutory submission remain separate external requirements.

Built to grow

Artha provides a connected operating layer for SMB accounting, POS, inventory, commerce and vertical business workflows. Its shared foundations let specialised operations remain tied to stock, customers, finance, permissions and audit evidence.
