Platform overview

A serious foundation for modern business operations.

Artha Business Suite is a multi-industry operating platform spanning sales, inventory, purchasing, finance and GST preparation, customers, teams, reporting, merchant commerce and specialised operational engines.

Release posture

The public Artha v1.0.9 package revision r4 remains an unsigned evaluation release. The canonical source now contains 124 migrations. Production is deliberately controlled through 2026110702 with 145/145 public tables under RLS and 198 policies; staging continues through 2026110712 with 156/156 RLS-protected tables and 201 policies. Production builds fail closed for healthcare operations, live transformation progress and commerce growth evidence until their migrations and external gates are complete. Publisher signing, clean-device acceptance, provider activation, healthcare review, hardware certification and statutory submission remain separate external requirements.

Built to grow

Artha provides a connected operating layer for SMB accounting, POS, inventory, commerce and vertical business workflows. Its shared foundations let specialised operations remain tied to stock, customers, finance, permissions and audit evidence.
