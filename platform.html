Platform overview

A serious foundation for modern business operations.

Artha Business Suite is a multi-industry operating platform spanning sales, inventory, purchasing, finance/GST preparation, customers, teams, reporting and merchant commerce. It is designed to let businesses work from one connected operational foundation.

Release posture

Artha describes product scope and release evidence clearly. Provider activation, hardware certification, platform signing, statutory submission and legal/company records are completed through their own controlled processes and are never implied merely by a screen or feature.

Built to grow

Artha provides a connected operating layer for SMB accounting, POS, inventory, commerce and vertical business workflows. Its configurable foundations create room for specialised industry workflows, integrations and expansion across sales channels.

Company materials

Artha’s company workspace holds the product documentation, architecture notes, release packages, test evidence, ownership/IP readiness records and approved brand media that support long-term product development.
